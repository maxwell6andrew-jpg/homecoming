Privacy

Last updated October 9, 2026 (ads added)

Short version: Homecoming has no accounts and no analytics. Your saves stay on your own device, and we don't collect your name, email or location. The game is free because it shows occasional ads from Google between games; Google may use cookies for those ads, as explained below.

What stays on your device

The game saves your Coach Mode career, settings (like sound on/off), tips you've already seen, your record against other schools and any school colors you fix. These are kept in your browser's local storage on your device only. They are never sent to us. You can erase them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Ads and cookies

Homecoming itself doesn't set cookies. To keep the game free, it shows ads through Google AdSense: at most one full-screen ad after a game ends, never during play. Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, and (where allowed and if you agree) to personalize them. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

  • You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party ad networks at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are used.
  • California residents: we don't sell your personal information ourselves; you can limit ad personalization with the links above.
  • Homecoming Pro (a one-time purchase, coming soon) removes all ads.

Who else is involved when you load the page

Like most websites, loading the game makes your browser contact a few services. They receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site (GitHub privacy statement).
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs (Cloudflare) serve open-source code libraries the game uses.
  • Google AdSense serves ads (see above).
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces (Google privacy policy).

We don't receive or keep those request logs, and we don't combine them with anything else.

Location

The game never asks for your location. You pick a school yourself; the field and neighborhood are drawn from public map data for that school.

Kids and teens

Homecoming is made for high school football fans. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone. Ads are served by Google under its policies; we don't knowingly direct the game at children under 13.
