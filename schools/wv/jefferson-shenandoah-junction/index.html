Shenandoah Junction, Jefferson County, West Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Jefferson High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Shenandoah Junction as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 9 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 4 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 50 m for the home crowd and 49 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
