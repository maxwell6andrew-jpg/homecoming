Ripley, Jackson County, West Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Ripley High School.

In the game, Ripley is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 31 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 44° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 84 m for the home crowd and 45 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
