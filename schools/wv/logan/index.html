The game sets Logan Senior High School in a north woods town, with dense pine woods. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 175 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 26 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 12° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 81 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
