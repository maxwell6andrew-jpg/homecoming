Homecoming builds Madison as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 83 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 16 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 115° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 79 m of seating on the home side and 45 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
