The game sets Issaquah High School in a mountain town, with tall pines and rock; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 53 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 14 streets and 110 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 167° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 38 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
