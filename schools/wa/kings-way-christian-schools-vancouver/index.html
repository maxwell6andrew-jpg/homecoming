The game sets Kings Way Christian Schools in a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 420 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 38 streets and 165 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 58 m of seating on the home side and 22 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
