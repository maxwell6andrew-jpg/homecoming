Tonasket, Okanogan County, Washington

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Tonasket High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Tonasket as a farm town with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 121 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 9 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 15° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 23 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
