Homecoming builds Spokane Valley as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 2 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 16 streets and 34 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 60 m for the home crowd and 43 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball.
