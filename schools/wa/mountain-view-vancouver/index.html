Homecoming builds Vancouver as a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 388 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 19 streets. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 165° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 103 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
