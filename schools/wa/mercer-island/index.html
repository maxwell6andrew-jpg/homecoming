In the game, Mercer Island is a beach suburb with open water to the north, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 276 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 35 streets and 84 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 89 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
