Homecoming builds Bellingham as a coastal city with a mountain view and open water to the west, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 130 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 21 streets. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 71° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 30 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
