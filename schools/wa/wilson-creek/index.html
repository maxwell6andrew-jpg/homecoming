Wilson Creek, Grant County, Washington

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Wilson Creek High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Wilson Creek High in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 166 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 14 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 15 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
