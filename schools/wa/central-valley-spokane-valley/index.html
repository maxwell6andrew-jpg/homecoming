Homecoming builds Spokane Valley as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 131 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 13 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 71° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 55 m for the home crowd and 34 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
