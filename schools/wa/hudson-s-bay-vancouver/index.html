Homecoming builds Vancouver as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 15 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 31 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 113° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 9 m for the home crowd and 8 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
