Ilwaco, Pacific County, Washington

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Ilwaco High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Ilwaco is a beach town with open water to the west, among pines and oaks. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 105 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 21 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 62 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
