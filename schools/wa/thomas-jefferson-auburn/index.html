In the game, Auburn is a beach suburb with open water to the west, among pines and oaks. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 287 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 25 streets. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
