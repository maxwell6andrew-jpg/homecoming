Mount Vernon, Skagit County, Washington

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Mount Vernon High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Mount Vernon as a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 238 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 27 streets and 51 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 60 m for the home crowd and 26 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
