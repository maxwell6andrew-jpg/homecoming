Homecoming builds Tacoma as a beach suburb with a mountain view and open water to the west, among pines and oaks. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 198 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 43 streets and 141 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 39 m of seating on the home side and 33 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
