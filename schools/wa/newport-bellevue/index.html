Homecoming builds Bellevue as a beach suburb with a mountain view and open water to the northwest, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 231 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 47 streets and 287 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 73 m of seating on the home side and 40 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
