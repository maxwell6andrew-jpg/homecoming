Trout Lake School football on your real field

Trout Lake, Klickitat County, Washington

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Trout Lake School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Trout Lake as the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 15 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 2 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 19 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
