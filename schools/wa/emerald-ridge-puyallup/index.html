Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Emerald Ridge High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Puyallup as a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 52 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 9 streets and 108 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 121° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
