Stanwood, Snohomish County, Washington

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Stanwood High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Stanwood as a beach town with a mountain view and open water to the northwest, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 4 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 16 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 81 m of seating on the home side and 49 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
