In the game, Kent is a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 143 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 26 streets and 53 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 29° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
