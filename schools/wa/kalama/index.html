In the game, Kalama is a mountain town, with tall pines and rock. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 135 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 15 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 137° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 51 m for the home crowd and 25 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
