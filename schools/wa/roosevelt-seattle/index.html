Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Roosevelt High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Roosevelt High School in a coastal city with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds a big-city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 66 streets and 29 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 28 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
