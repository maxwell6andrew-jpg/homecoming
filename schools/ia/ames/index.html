The game sets Ames High School in a city, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 26 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 4 streets and 25 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 110° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
