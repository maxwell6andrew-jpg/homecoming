Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with John F Kennedy High School.

Homecoming builds Cedar Rapids as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 234 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 9 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 26 m for the home crowd and 5 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
