Spirit Lake, Dickinson County, Iowa

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Spirit Lake High School.

Homecoming builds Spirit Lake as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 91 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 15 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 70 m of seating on the home side and 93 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
