Davenport, Scott County, Iowa

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with West High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Davenport is set in hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 74 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 125° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
