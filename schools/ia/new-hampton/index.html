New Hampton, Chickasaw County, Iowa

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with New Hampton High School.

The game sets New Hampton High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 44 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 40 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 165° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 55 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
