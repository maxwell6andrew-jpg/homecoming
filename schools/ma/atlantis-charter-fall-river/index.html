The game sets Atlantis Charter School in coastal hillside estates with open water to the northwest, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 96 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 6 streets and 56 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 135 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 15° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 28 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
