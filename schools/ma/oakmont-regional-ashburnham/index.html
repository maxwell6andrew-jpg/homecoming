Ashburnham, Worcester County, Massachusetts

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Oakmont Regional High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Oakmont Regional High School in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 12 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 2 streets and 67 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 160° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 25 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
