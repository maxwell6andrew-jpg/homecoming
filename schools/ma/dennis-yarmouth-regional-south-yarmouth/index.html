Homecoming builds South Yarmouth as a beach suburb with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 223 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 13 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 33° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 45 m for the home crowd and 44 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
