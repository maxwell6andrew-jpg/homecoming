Spencer, Worcester County, Massachusetts

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with David Prouty High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets David Prouty High in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 112 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 7 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 56° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 25 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
