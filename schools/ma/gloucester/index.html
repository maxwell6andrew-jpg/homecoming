The game sets Gloucester High in a beach suburb with open water to the southeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 355 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 36 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 78° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 86 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
