Boston, Suffolk County, Massachusetts

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with English High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Boston as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (mostly houses) and 49 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 126° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 63 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
