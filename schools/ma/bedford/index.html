In the game, Bedford is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 103 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 27 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 120° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 45 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
