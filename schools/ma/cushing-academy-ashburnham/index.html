Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Cushing Academy.

Homecoming builds Ashburnham as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 159 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 9 streets and 166 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 27 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
