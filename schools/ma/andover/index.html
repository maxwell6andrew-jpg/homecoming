In the game, Andover is set in coastal hillside estates with open water to the southeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 55 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 4 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 12° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 93 m of seating on the home side and 29 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
