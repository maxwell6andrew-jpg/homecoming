The game sets Carter School in a coastal city with open water to the east, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds a big-city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 81 streets and 32 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 132° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
