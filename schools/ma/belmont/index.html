Belmont, Middlesex County, Massachusetts

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Belmont High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Belmont as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 30 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 77° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
