In the game, Maynard is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 172 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 111° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 49 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
