The game sets Billerica Memorial High School in a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 167 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 26 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 125 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 163° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 62 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
