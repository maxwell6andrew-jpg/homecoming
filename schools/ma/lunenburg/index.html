Lunenburg, Worcester County, Massachusetts

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lunenburg High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Lunenburg as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 136 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 17 streets and 162 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 73° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 9 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
