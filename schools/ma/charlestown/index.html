Homecoming builds Charlestown as a beach suburb with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 429 buildings (mostly houses) and 32 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 119° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
