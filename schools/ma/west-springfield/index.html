West Springfield High football on your real field

West Springfield, Hampden County, Massachusetts

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with West Springfield High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds West Springfield as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 97 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 4 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 12° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 88 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
