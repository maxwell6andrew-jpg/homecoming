Dorchester, Suffolk County, Massachusetts

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Boston College High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Dorchester as a coastal city with open water to the east, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see a big-city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 47 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 53 streets and 216 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 158° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 49 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
