Homecoming builds Harwich as coastal hillside estates with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 74 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 9 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 146° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
