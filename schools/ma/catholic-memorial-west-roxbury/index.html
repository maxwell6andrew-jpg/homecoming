West Roxbury, Suffolk County, Massachusetts

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Catholic Memorial.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds West Roxbury as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 45 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 70° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
