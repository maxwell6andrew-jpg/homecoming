Homecoming builds Worcester as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 167 buildings (mostly houses) and 12 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 118° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 67 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
