In the game, Omaha is a city, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 205 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 11 streets and 233 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 139° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 67 m of seating on the home side and 26 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
