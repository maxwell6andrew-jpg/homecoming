Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lincoln High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Lincoln is a big city, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see a big-city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 314 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses), 85 streets and 234 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 60 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
