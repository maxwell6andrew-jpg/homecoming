Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Garden County Junior/Senior High School.

Homecoming builds Oshkosh as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 323 buildings (mostly houses) and 15 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 28 m for the home crowd and 10 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
