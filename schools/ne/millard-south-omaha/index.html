Omaha, Douglas County, Nebraska

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Millard South High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Omaha as a city, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 308 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 28 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 90 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
