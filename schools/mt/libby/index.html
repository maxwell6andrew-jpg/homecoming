Homecoming builds Libby as a mountain town, with tall pines and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 25 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 9 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 120 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 167° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
