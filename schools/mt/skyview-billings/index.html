The game sets Skyview High School in hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 68 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 7 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 106° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
