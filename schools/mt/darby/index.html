Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Darby High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Darby as a mountain town, with tall pines and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 61 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 10 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
