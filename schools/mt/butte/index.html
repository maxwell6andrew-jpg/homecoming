Homecoming builds Butte as a mountain town, with tall pines and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 287 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 68 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 109 m for the home crowd and 42 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
