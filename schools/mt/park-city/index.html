Park City, Stillwater County, Montana

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Park City High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Park City High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 40 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 63° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
