The game sets Merritt Island High School in a beach suburb with open water to the east, among palms. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 204 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 26 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 99 m of seating on the home side and 52 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
