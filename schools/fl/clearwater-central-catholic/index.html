Homecoming builds Clearwater as a beach suburb with open water to the east, among palms. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 240 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 13 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 67 m of seating on the home side and 22 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
