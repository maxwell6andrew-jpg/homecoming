Homecoming builds Naples as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 220 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 11 streets and 94 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 140° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 98 m of seating on the home side and 43 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
