Homecoming builds Miami as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 195 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 7 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 144° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 15 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
