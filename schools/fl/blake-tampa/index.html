The game sets Blake High School in a coastal city with open water to the southwest, among palms; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 26 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 56 streets and 104 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 86 m of seating on the home side and 87 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
