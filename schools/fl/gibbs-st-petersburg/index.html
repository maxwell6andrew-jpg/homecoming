Homecoming builds St. Petersburg as a beach suburb with open water to the southwest, among palms. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 141 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 44 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 54 m of seating on the home side and 32 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
