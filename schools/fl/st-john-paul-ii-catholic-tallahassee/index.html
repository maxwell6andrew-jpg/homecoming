Homecoming builds Tallahassee as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 24 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 3 streets and 31 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 165° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 72 m for the home crowd and 54 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
