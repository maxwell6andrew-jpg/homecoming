Homecoming builds Venice as a coastal city with open water to the west, among palms; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 271 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 107° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 60 m for the home crowd and 65 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
