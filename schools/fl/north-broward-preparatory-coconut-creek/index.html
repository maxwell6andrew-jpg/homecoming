Homecoming builds Coconut Creek as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 126 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 40 streets and 94 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
