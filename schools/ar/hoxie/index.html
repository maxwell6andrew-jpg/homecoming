Homecoming builds Hoxie as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 20 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 16 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 121° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 46 m for the home crowd and 28 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
