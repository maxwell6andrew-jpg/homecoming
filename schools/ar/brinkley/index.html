Homecoming builds Brinkley as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap shows 1 street around the field but no buildings yet, so in the game that street runs past empty lots.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 60 m for the home crowd and 37 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
