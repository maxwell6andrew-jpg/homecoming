Sheridan, Grant County, Arkansas

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Sheridan High School.

Homecoming builds Sheridan as a small town, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 5 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 14 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 71 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
