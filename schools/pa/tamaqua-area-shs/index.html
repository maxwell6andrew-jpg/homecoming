In the game, Tamaqua is a north woods town, with dense pine woods. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 273 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 13 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 77° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 76 m for the home crowd and 65 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
