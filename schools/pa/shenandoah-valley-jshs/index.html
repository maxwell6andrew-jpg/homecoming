In the game, Shenandoah is a north woods town, with dense pine woods. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 9 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 20 streets and 35 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 166° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 52 m for the home crowd and 42 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
