The game sets Avonworth High School in hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 90 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 11 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
