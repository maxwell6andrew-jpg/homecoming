Homecoming builds East Stroudsburg as a small town, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 227 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 17 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 135 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 105° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 124 m of seating on the home side and 77 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
