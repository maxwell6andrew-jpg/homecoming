In the game, Renovo is a mountain town, with tall pines and rock. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 24 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 35° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 46 m for the home crowd and 43 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
