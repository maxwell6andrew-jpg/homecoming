York Springs, Adams County, Pennsylvania

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Bermudian Springs High School.

In the game, York Springs is a country town, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 16 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 2 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 137° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 33 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
