Taylor, Lackawanna County, Pennsylvania

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Riverside JSHS.

Homecoming builds Taylor as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 3 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 15 streets and 42 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 28° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
