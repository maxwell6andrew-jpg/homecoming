Homecoming builds Pottsville as a small town, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 15 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 14 streets and 93 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 67° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 90 m of seating on the home side and 76 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
