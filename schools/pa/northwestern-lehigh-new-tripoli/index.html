Homecoming builds New Tripoli as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 43 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 2 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 46 m of seating on the home side and 86 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
