Fort Washington, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Germantown Academy.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Fort Washington as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 3 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 129° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 52 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
