Homecoming builds Williamsburg as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 9 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 120 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 123° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
