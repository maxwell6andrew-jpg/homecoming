Eagleville, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Methacton High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Eagleville is a country town, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 3 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 2 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 77° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 50 m for the home crowd and 20 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
