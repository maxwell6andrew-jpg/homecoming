Homecoming builds Tunkhannock as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 31 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 13 streets and 73 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 22° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 76 m for the home crowd and 68 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
