Homecoming builds Wingate as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 78 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 2 streets. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 65° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 59 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
