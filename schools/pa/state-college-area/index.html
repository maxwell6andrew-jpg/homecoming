State College, Centre County, Pennsylvania

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with State College Area High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, State College is a city, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 130 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 12 streets and 66 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 54° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 14 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
