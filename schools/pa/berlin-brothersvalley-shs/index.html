In the game, Berlin is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 230 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 23 streets and 141 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 12° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 54 m for the home crowd and 32 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
