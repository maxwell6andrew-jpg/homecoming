Wyncote, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Cheltenham High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Wyncote as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 113 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 34 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 159° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
