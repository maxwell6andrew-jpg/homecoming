Homecoming builds Philadelphia as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 24 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 37° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
