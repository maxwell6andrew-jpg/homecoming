Fairless Hills, Bucks County, Pennsylvania

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Pennsbury High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Fairless Hills is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 173 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 10 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 120 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 13° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 45 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
