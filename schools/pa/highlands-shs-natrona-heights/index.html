Homecoming builds Natrona Heights as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 424 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 17 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 57° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 88 m of seating on the home side and 72 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
