The game sets North Bergen High School in a beach suburb with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 57 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 31° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
