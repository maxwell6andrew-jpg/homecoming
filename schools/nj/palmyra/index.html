Homecoming builds Palmyra as coastal hillside estates with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 137 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 33 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 141° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 77 m for the home crowd and 20 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
