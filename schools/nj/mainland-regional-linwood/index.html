Homecoming builds Linwood as a beach suburb with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 11 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 18 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 32° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 53 m of seating on the home side and 44 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
