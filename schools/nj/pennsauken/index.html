Homecoming builds Pennsauken as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 21 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 129° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 49 m for the home crowd and 35 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
