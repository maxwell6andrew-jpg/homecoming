Homecoming builds Gloucester City as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 52 buildings (mostly houses) and 44 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 38° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
