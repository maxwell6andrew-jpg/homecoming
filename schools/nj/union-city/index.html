Union City, Hudson County, New Jersey

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Union City High School.

Homecoming builds Union City as a beach suburb with open water to the north, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (mostly houses) and 59 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 5 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 122° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 91 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
