In the game, Jersey City is a coastal city with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. Past the real neighbourhood you see a big-city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 438 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 72 streets and 139 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 13 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
