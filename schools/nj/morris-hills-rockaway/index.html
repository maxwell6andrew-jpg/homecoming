Homecoming builds Rockaway as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 256 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 14 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 41° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 42 m for the home crowd and 34 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
