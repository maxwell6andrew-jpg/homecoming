The game sets Ocean City High School in a beach suburb with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 36 buildings (mostly houses) and 14 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 135° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 54 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
