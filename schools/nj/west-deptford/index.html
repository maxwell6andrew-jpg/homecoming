The game sets West Deptford High School in a beach suburb with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 126 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 14 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 115° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 63 m for the home crowd and 104 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
