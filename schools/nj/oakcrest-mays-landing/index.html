The game sets Oakcrest High School in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 9 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 1 street. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 65° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 70 m for the home crowd and 46 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
