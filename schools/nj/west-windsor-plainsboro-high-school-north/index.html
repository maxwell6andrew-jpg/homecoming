In the game, Plainsboro is a country town, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 2 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 2 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 105° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 48 m for the home crowd and 46 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
