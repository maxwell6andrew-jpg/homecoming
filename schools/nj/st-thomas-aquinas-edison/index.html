Homecoming builds Edison as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 7 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 14 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 38° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
