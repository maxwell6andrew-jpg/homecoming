In the game, Pleasantville is set in coastal hillside estates with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 71 buildings (mostly houses) and 6 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
