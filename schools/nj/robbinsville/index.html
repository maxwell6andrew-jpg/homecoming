The game sets Robbinsville High School in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 12 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 151° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 48 m of seating on the home side and 27 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
