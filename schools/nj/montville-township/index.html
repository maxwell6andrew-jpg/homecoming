In the game, Montville is set in hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 60 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 10 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
