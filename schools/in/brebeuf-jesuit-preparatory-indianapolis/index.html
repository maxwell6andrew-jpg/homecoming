Homecoming builds Indianapolis as a city, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 43 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 33 streets and 186 individually mapped trees.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
