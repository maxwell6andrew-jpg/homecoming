The game sets Batesville High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 18 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 30 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
