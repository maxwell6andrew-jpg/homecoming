In the game, Indianapolis is a city, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 70 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 16 streets. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 92 m for the home crowd and 95 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
