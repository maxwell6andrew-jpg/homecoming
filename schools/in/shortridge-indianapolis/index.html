In the game, Indianapolis is a city, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 374 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 35 streets and 30 individually mapped trees. The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
