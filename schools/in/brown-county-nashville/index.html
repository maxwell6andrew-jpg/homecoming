In the game, Nashville is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 32 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 15 streets and 83 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 23° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 62 m for the home crowd and 29 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
