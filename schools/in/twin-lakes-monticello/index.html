Homecoming builds Monticello as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 70 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 20 streets. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
