The game sets Arsenal Technical High School in a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 387 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 28 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 123 m of seating on the home side and 123 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
