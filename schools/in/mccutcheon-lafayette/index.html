The game sets McCutcheon High School in a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 82 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 166° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
