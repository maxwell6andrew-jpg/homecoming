Brooksville, Bracken County, Kentucky

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Bracken County High School.

Homecoming builds Brooksville as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: only 5 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 2 streets. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 33 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
