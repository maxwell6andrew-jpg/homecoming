Homecoming builds Somerset as a small town, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 14 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 15 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 164° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 78 m of seating on the home side and 20 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
