Fort Mitchell, Kenton County, Kentucky

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Beechwood High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Fort Mitchell is set in hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 107 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 26 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 30° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 62 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
