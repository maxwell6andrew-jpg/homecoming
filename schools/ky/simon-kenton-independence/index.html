Independence, Kenton County, Kentucky

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Simon Kenton High School.

Homecoming builds Independence as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 75 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 14 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 161° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 55 m for the home crowd and 23 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
