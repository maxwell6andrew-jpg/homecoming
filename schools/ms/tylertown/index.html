Homecoming builds Tylertown as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 12 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 5 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 104° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
