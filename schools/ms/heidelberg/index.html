Heidelberg, Jasper County, Mississippi

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Heidelberg High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Heidelberg High School in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 12 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 6 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 51 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
