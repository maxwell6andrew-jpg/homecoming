Shawnee, Johnson County, Kansas

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Maranatha Christian Academy.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Shawnee as hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 69 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 15 streets and 350 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 44 m of seating on the home side and 22 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
