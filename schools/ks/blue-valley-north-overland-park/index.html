Overland Park, Johnson County, Kansas

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Blue Valley North High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Overland Park is a city, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 32 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 14 streets and 60 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 54 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
