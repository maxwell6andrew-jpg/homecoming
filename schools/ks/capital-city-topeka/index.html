Topeka, Shawnee County, Kansas

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Capital City.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Topeka as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 138 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 17 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 135 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 89 m for the home crowd and 87 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
