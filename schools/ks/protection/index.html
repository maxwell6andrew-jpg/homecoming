Protection, Comanche County, Kansas

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Protection High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Protection as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 128 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 12 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 24 m of seating on the home side and 10 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
