Homecoming builds Kansas City as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 425 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses), 26 streets and 31 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 121 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
