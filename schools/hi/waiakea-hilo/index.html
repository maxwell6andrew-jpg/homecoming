Homecoming builds Hilo as a beach town with a mountain view and open water to the north, among palms. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 271 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 6 streets and 106 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 69 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
