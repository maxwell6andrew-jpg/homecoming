Kaneohe, Honolulu County, Hawaii

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Castle High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Kaneohe is a beach suburb with open water to the north, among palms. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 127 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 28 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 150° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 99 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
