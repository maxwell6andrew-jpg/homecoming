Panaca, Lincoln County, Nevada

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lincoln County High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Panaca is set in desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 267 buildings (mostly houses), 11 streets and 95 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 62 m for the home crowd and 15 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
