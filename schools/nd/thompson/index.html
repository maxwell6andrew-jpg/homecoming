Thompson, Grand Forks County, North Dakota

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Thompson High School.

Homecoming builds Thompson as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 387 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 24 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 35 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 28 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
