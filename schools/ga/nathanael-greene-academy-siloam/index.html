Homecoming builds Siloam as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 1 building within about 400 m of midfield and 2 streets. The game puts it around the stadium. The closest building is about 140 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 77° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
