Homecoming builds Chickamauga as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 206 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 12 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 107° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 78 m for the home crowd and 51 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
