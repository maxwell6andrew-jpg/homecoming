Homecoming builds Norcross as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 129 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 33 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 34° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 90 m for the home crowd and 91 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
