In the game, Athens is a city, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 263 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 46 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 165° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 81 m of seating on the home side and 66 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
