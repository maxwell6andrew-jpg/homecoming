Homecoming builds Thomasville as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 61 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 17 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 142° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
