Homecoming builds Woodstock as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 100 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 26 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 130° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 43 m for the home crowd and 94 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
