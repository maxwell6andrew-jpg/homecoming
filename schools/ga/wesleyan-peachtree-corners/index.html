Peachtree Corners, Gwinnett County, Georgia

In the game, Peachtree Corners is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 97 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 31 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 40 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
