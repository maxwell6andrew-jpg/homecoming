Homecoming builds Columbus as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 8 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 3 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 60° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 37 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
