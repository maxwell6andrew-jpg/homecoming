Evans, Columbia County, Georgia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lakeside High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Evans as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 139 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 10 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 24 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
