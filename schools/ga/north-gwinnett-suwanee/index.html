The game sets North Gwinnett High School in a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 124 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 15 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 160° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 46 m of seating on the home side and 37 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
