The game sets Stratford Academy in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 22 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 6 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 48° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 51 m of seating on the home side and 53 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
