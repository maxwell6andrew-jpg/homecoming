The game sets Southwest Georgia Academy in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap shows 2 streets around the field but no buildings yet, so in the game those streets run past empty lots.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball.
