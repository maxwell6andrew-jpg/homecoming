Homecoming builds Roseville as hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 82 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 18 streets. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 74 m of seating on the home side and 38 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
