Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Osseo Senior High.

Homecoming builds Osseo as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 20 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 11 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 27° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
