Cottage Grove, Washington County, Minnesota

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Park High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Cottage Grove as hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 27 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 25 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 134° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 74 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
