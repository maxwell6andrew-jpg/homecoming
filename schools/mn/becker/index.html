Becker Senior High football on your real field

Becker, Sherburne County, Minnesota

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Becker Senior High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Becker Senior High in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 66 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 7 streets and 26 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
