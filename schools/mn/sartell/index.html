Sartell, Stearns County, Minnesota

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Sartell Senior High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Sartell Senior High in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 64 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 5 streets and 44 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
