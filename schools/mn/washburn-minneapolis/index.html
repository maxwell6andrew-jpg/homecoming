Minneapolis, Hennepin County, Minnesota

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Washburn High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Minneapolis is a city, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 40 streets and 34 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 75 m for the home crowd and 83 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
