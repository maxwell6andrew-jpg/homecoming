In the game, Spring Lake Park is a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 1 building within about 400 m of midfield and 15 streets. The game puts it around the stadium.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 22° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 86 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
