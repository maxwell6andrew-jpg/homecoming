Nashwauk, Itasca County, Minnesota

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Nashwauk Secondary.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Nashwauk Secondary in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 243 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 11 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 42 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
