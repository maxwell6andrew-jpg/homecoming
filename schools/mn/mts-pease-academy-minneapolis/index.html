The game sets Mts Pease Academy in a city, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 232 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 32 streets and 27 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 119° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
