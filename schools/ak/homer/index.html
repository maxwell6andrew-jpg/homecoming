Homer High School football on your real field

Homer, Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Homer High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Homer High School in a beach town with a mountain view and open water to the southwest, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 66 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 15 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 65° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
