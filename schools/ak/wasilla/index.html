Wasilla, Matanuska-Susitna Borough, Alaska

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Wasilla High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Wasilla High School in a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 110 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 8 streets and 29 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 50 m for the home crowd and 36 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
