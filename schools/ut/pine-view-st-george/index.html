St. George, Washington County, Utah

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Pine View High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds St. George as a desert city with a mountain view, among palms and tall cactus; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 164 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 21 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
