The game sets Kanab High in desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 117 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 14 streets. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 38 m for the home crowd and 37 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
