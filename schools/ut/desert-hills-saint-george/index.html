Saint George, Washington County, Utah

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Desert Hills High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Saint George as desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 102 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 93 m for the home crowd and 39 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
