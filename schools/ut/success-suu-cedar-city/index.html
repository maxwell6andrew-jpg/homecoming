Homecoming builds Cedar City as a desert town with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 166 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 25 streets. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 102 m of seating on the home side and 100 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
