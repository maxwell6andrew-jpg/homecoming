The game sets Uintah High in a mountain town, with tall pines and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 88 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 6 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 78 m of seating on the home side and 60 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
