Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Highland High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Highland High in a foothill suburb, with tall pines and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 193 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 35 streets and 225 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 59 m for the home crowd and 31 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
