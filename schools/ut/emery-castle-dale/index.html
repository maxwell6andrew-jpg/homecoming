The game sets Emery High in a country town with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 102 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 15 streets. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 81 m of seating on the home side and 93 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
