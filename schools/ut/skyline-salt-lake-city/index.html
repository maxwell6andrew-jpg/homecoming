Homecoming builds Salt Lake City as a foothill suburb, with tall pines and rock. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 226 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 46 streets.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 114 m of seating on the home side and 49 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
