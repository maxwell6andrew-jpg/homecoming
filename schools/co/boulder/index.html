Boulder, Boulder County, Colorado

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Boulder High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Boulder is a mountain city, with tall pines and rock. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 238 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 113 streets and 143 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 85 m for the home crowd and 56 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
