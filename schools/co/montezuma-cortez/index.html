Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Montezuma-Cortez High School.

Homecoming builds Cortez as desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 5 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 4 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 161° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
