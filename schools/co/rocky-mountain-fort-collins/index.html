Homecoming builds Fort Collins as a city with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 292 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 29 streets. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 71 m for the home crowd and 73 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
