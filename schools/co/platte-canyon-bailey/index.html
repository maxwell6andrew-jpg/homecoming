Homecoming builds Bailey as a mountain town, with tall pines and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 4 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 0 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 114° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 40 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
