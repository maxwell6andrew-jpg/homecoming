Golden, Jefferson County, Colorado

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Golden High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Golden High School in a foothill suburb, with tall pines and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 242 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 25 streets and 199 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 14 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
