In the game, Highlands Ranch is set in hillside estates with a mountain view, among dry brush, oaks and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 21 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
