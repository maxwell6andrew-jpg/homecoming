Peyton, El Paso County, Colorado

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Falcon High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Peyton is a farm town with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 52 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
