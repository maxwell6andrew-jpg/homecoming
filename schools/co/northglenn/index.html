Northglenn, Adams County, Colorado

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Northglenn High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Northglenn High School in a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 229 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 17 streets and 233 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 151° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 28 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
