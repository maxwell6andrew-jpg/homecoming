Arvada, Jefferson County, Colorado

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Pomona Junior/Senior High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Arvada as a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 325 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 31 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
