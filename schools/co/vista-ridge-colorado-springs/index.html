Homecoming builds Colorado Springs as a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 137 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 10 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 23° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 43 m for the home crowd and 24 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
