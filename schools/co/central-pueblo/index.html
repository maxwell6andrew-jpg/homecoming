Pueblo, Pueblo County, Colorado

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Central High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Pueblo is set in city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 351 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 21 streets and 242 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 45° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 29 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
