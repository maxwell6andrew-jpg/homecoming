Homecoming builds Castle Rock as hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 91 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 152° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
