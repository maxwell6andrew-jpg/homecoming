The game sets Niwot High School in ranch hills with a mountain view, among dry brush, oaks and rock; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 58 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 17 streets. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 139° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 22 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
