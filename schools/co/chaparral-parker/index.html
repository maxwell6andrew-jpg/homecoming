Homecoming builds Parker as a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 185 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 18 streets. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 153° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 22 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
