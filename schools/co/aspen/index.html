Aspen, Pitkin County, Colorado

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Aspen High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Aspen as a mountain town, with tall pines and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 26 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 3 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 39 m of seating on the home side and 19 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
