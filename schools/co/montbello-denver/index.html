The game sets Montbello High School in city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 398 buildings (mostly houses) and 29 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 41 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
