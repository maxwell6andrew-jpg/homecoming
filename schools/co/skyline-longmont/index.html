The game sets Skyline High School in a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 304 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 23 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 151° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 21 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
