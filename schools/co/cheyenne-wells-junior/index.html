The game sets Cheyenne Wells Junior/High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
