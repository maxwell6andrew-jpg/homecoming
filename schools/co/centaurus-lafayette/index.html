Homecoming builds Lafayette as a suburb with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 241 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 47 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 115° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 66 m for the home crowd and 68 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
