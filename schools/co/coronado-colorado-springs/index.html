In the game, Colorado Springs is set in mountain estates, with tall pines and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 94 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 18 streets and 113 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 17° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
