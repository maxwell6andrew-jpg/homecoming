Homecoming builds Pueblo as a suburb with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 11 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 22 streets and 100 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 52° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 39 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
