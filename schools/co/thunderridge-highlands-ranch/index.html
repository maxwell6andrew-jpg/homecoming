In the game, Highlands Ranch is set in hillside estates with a mountain view, among dry brush, oaks and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 73 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 21 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 152° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
