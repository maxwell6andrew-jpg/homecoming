Chantilly, Fairfax County, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Westfield High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Chantilly as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 14 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 6 streets and 168 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 164° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 33 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
