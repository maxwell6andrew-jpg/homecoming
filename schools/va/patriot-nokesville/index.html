Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Patriot High.

The game sets Patriot High in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 25 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 1 street. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 33° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 82 m of seating on the home side and 51 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
