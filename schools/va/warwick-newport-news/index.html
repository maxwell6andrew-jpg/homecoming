The game sets Warwick High in a coastal city with open water to the southwest, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 255 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 25 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 135° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 5 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
