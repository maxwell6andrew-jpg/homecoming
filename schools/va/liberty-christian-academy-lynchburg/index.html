The game sets Liberty Christian Academy in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 17 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 41 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 35° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 78 m of seating on the home side and 40 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
