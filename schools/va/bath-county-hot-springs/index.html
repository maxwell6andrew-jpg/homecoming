Hot Springs, Bath County, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Bath County High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Hot Springs as a mountain town, with tall pines and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 4 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 32° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
