Homecoming builds Onley as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: only 2 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 9 streets. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 134° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 44 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
