Arlington, Arlington County, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Yorktown High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Arlington is a city, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 28 streets and 35 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 66° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
