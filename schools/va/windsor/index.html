Windsor, Isle of Wight County, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Windsor High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Windsor High in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 166 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 9 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 32 m for the home crowd and 19 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
