In the game, Virginia Beach is a coastal city with open water to the west, among pines and oaks. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 265 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 20 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 22° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
