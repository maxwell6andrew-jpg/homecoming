Homecoming builds Newport News as a coastal city with open water to the west, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 431 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 14 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 153° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 21 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
