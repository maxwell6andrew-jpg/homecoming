In the game, Chesterfield is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 16 buildings (mostly houses), 1 street and 92 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 103° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 76 m of seating on the home side and 50 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
