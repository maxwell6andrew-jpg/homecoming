Stephens City, Frederick County, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Sherando High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Stephens City is a country town, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 11 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 29° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 24 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
