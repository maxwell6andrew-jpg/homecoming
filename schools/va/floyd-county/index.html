In the game, Floyd is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 10 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 4 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 167° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 47 m of seating on the home side and 36 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
