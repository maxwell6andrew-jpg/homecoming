The game sets Centreville High in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 86 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 7 streets and 153 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 59° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 101 m of seating on the home side and 101 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
