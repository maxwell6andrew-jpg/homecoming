Lee High football on your real field

Jonesville, Lee County, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lee High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Lee High in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 5 buildings (mostly houses) and 3 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 62° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 94 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
