In the game, Chantilly is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 252 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 30 streets and 90 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 26° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 76 m of seating on the home side and 81 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
