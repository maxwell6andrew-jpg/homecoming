In the game, Gate City is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 12 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 25 streets. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 168 m of seating on the home side and 120 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
