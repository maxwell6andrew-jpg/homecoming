The game sets Fairfax High in a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 175 buildings (mostly houses), 37 streets and 132 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 92 m for the home crowd and 78 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
