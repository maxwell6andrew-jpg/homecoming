The game sets West Potomac High in a beach suburb with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 254 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 15 streets and 56 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 69° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 76 m for the home crowd and 45 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
