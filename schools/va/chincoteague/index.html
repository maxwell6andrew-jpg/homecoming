Chincoteague, Accomack County, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Chincoteague High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Chincoteague as a beach town with open water to the west, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 27 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 10 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 140 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 67° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 29 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
