Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Forest Park High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Forest Park High in coastal hillside estates with open water to the southeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 187 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 14 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 73° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
