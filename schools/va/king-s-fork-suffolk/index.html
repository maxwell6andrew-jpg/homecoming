In the game, Suffolk is a small town, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 147 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 17 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 22° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 139 m for the home crowd and 54 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
