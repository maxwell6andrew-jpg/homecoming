The game sets Booker T Washington High in a city, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 295 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 56 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 112° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
