In the game, Crozet is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 2 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 51 m for the home crowd and 27 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
