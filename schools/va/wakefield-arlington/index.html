Arlington, Arlington County, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Wakefield High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Arlington is a city, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 420 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 35 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 160° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 53 m for the home crowd and 32 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
