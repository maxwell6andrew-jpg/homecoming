Alexandria, Alexandria city, Virginia

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Alexandria City High.

In the game, Alexandria is a coastal city with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 200 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 39 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 130° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 52 m of seating on the home side and 34 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
