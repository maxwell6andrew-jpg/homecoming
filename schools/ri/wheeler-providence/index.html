Providence, Providence County, Rhode Island

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Wheeler School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Providence as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 20 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 157° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 45 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
