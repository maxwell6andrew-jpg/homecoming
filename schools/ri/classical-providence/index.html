Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Classical High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Classical High School in a city, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 226 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 106 streets and 129 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 40 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 59° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 28 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
