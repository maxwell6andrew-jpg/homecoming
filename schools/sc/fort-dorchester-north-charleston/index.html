Homecoming builds North Charleston as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 125 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 36° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 17 m for the home crowd and 14 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
