Florence, Florence County, South Carolina

In the game, Florence is a country town, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 1 building and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 156° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 92 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
