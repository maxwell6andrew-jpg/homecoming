Graniteville, Aiken County, South Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Midland Valley High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Graniteville as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 4 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 4 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 135° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 85 m for the home crowd and 41 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
