Spartanburg, Spartanburg County, South Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Broome High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Spartanburg as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 59 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 11 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 74 m of seating on the home side and 90 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
