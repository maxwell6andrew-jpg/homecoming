Chesnee, Spartanburg County, South Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Chesnee High.

The game sets Chesnee High in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 41 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 6 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 35 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 104° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 85 m for the home crowd and 43 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
