The game sets Socastee High in a beach suburb with open water to the east, among palms. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 150 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 22 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 57° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 66 m for the home crowd and 57 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
