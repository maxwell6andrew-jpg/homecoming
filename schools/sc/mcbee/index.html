Homecoming builds McBee as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 22 buildings (mostly houses) and 7 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 72° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 37 m for the home crowd and 35 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
