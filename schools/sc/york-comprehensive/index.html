York, York County, South Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with York Comprehensive High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds York as a small town, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 11 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 15 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 43 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
