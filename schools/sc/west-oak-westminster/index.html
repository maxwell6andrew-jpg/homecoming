Westminster, Oconee County, South Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with West-Oak High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Westminster as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 1 building within about 400 m of midfield and 4 streets. The game puts it around the stadium.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 92 m for the home crowd and 35 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
