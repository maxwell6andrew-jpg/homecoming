Homecoming builds Sutherlin as a north woods town with a mountain view, among dense pine woods. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 315 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 77° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
