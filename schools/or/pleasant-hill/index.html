The game sets Pleasant Hill High School in the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 44 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 3 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 136° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
