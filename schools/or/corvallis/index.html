Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Corvallis High School.

The game sets Corvallis High School in city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (mostly houses), 25 streets and 226 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 107° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 83 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
