Homecoming builds Portland as city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; past the real streets you see a big-city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 234 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 158 streets and 196 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 111° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 47 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
