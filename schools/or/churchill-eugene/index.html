The game sets Churchill High School in a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 318 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 30 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 40 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
