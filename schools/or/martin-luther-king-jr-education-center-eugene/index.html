Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Martin Luther King Jr. Education Center.

In the game, Eugene is set in city in the hills with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 30 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 4 streets and 79 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 105° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
