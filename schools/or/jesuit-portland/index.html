Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Jesuit High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Jesuit High School in city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 347 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 31 streets and 58 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 119 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
