Homecoming builds Nyssa as a desert town, with cactus, desert brush and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 221 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 10 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 46° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 45 m for the home crowd and 31 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
