The game sets Condon High School in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 182 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 21 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 163° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
