Homecoming builds Beaverton as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 109 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses), 29 streets and 350 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 68 m for the home crowd and 56 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
