In the game, Gaston is set in the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 269 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 27 streets and 54 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 35 m from midfield.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
