Homecoming builds Eugene as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 214 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 8 streets. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 45 m of seating on the home side and 50 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
