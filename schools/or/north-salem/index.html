Homecoming builds Salem as a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 313 buildings (mostly houses) and 43 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 20° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 82 m for the home crowd and 97 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
