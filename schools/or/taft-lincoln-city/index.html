The game sets Taft High School in a beach town with a mountain view and open water to the west, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 103 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 17 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 156° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 40 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
