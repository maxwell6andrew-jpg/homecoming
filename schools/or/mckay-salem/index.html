The game sets McKay High School in a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 343 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 21 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 135° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
