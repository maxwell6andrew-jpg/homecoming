Homecoming builds Portland as city in the hills with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 325 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 32 streets and 155 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 53 m of seating on the home side and 104 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
