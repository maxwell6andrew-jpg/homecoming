In the game, Sherwood is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 27 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 25 streets and 350 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 120° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 59 m of seating on the home side and 30 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
