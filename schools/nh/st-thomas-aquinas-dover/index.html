The game sets St. Thomas Aquinas High School in the north woods, with dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 3 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 9 streets and 47 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 166° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 42 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
