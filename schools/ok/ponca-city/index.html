Homecoming builds Ponca City as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 17 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 18 streets. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 99 m for the home crowd and 92 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
