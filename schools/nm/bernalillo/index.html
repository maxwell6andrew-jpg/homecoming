The game sets Bernalillo High in a desert suburb with a mountain view, among palms and tall cactus. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 15 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 12 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 22° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
