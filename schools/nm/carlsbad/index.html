The game sets Carlsbad High in a desert town, with cactus, desert brush and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 191 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 5 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 158° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 70 m for the home crowd and 56 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
