Homecoming builds Farmington as desert estates, with cactus, desert brush and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 151 buildings (mostly houses) and 8 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 160° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 34 m of seating on the home side and 30 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
