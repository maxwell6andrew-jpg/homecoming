Carrizozo, Lincoln County, New Mexico

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Carrizozo High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Carrizozo is set in desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 210 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 24 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
