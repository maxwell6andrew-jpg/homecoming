Newcomb, San Juan County, New Mexico

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Newcomb High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Newcomb is set in desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 2 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 14° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
