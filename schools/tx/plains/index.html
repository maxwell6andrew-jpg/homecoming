Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Plains High School.

The game sets Plains High School in desert ranch country, with cactus, desert brush and rock; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 26 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 14 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
