The game sets Byron Nelson High School in a canyon suburb, with palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 128 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 7 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 22° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 36 m for the home crowd and 28 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
