The game sets East Bernard High School in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 202 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 19 streets and 49 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 73° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 62 m for the home crowd and 37 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
