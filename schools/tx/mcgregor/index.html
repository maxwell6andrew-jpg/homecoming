The game sets McGregor High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 9 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 3 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 148° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 58 m of seating on the home side and 55 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
