The game sets Marfa Schools in desert ranch country, with cactus, desert brush and rock; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 254 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 31 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 162° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 81 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
