In the game, Rio Vista is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 71 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 7 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 151° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 43 m for the home crowd and 37 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
