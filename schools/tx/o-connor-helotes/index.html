In the game, Helotes is a canyon suburb, with palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 43 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 6 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 160° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 29 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
