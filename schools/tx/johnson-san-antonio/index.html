The game sets Johnson High School in a canyon suburb, with palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 34 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 9 streets and 39 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 137° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 50 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
