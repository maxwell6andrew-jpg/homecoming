Homecoming builds Von Ormy as a farm town: around the stadium, a country crossroads with a few farmsteads, barns, silos and crop fields, among oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap has the field but nothing mapped around it yet, so the game builds the neighbourhood from the town type.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 15° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
