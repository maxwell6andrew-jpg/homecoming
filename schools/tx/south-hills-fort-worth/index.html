Homecoming builds Fort Worth as a city, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 320 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 33 streets. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 130° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
