The game sets W T White High School in a canyon suburb, with palms, oaks and brush. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 205 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 8 streets. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
