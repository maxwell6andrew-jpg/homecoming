Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Harvest Christian Academy.

Homecoming builds Keller as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 264 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 16 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 13° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 55 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
