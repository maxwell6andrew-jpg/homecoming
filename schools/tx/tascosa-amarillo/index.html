Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Tascosa High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Tascosa High School in a city, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 191 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 40 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
