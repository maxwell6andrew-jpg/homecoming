The game sets Robert Lee School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 182 buildings (mostly houses) and 16 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 121° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 53 m of seating on the home side and 60 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
