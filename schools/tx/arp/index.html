Arp, Smith County, Texas

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Arp High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Arp as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 97 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 64 m for the home crowd and 71 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
