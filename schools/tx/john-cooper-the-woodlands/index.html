In the game, The Woodlands is set in hillside estates, with dry brush, oaks and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 74 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 30 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 21° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 59 m of seating on the home side and 29 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
