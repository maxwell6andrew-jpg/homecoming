Homecoming builds Priddy as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 17 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 156° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
