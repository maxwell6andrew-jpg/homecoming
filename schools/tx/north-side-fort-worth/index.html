The game sets North Side High School in a city, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 385 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 15 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 45° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
