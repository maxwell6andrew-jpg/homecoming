Homecoming builds College Station as city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 106 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 24 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 134° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 24 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
