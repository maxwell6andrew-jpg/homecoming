Homecoming builds Cibolo as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 59 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 17 streets. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 149° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 46 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
