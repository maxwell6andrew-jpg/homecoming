In the game, Muleshoe is a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 4 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 11 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 27° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 44 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
