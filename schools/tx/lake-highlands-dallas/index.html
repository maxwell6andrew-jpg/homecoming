Homecoming builds Dallas as city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; past the real streets you see a big-city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 138 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 29 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 101 m for the home crowd and 109 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
