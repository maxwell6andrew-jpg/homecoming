In the game, Fort Worth is set in hillside estates, with dry brush, oaks and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 239 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 22 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 17° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 52 m for the home crowd and 14 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
