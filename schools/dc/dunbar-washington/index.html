Homecoming builds Washington as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 450 or more buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses), 78 streets and 52 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 61 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
