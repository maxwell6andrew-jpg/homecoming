Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with New Albany High School.

The game sets New Albany High School in hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 42 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 9 streets and 62 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 49 m for the home crowd and 77 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
