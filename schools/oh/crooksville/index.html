The game sets Crooksville High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap shows 5 streets around the field but no buildings yet, so in the game those streets run past empty lots.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 60° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
