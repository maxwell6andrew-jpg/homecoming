The game sets Triway High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 9 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 3 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 138° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 50 m for the home crowd and 37 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
