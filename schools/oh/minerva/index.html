Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Minerva High School.

The game sets Minerva High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 4 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 14 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 145° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
