The game sets Hawken School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 16 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 0 streets and 120 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 39 m of seating on the home side and 22 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
