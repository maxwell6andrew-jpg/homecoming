In the game, Hilliard is a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 79 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 29 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 135 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball.
