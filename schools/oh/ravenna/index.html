Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Ravenna High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Ravenna is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 1 building within about 400 m of midfield and 1 street. The game puts it around the stadium.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 155° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
