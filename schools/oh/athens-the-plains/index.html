The Plains, Athens County, Ohio

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Athens High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds The Plains as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 209 buildings (mostly houses) and 7 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 71° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 14 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
