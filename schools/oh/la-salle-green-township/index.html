Homecoming builds Green Township as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 333 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 9 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 20° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 93 m of seating on the home side and 92 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
