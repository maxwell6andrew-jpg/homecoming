Homecoming builds Galloway as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 10 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 18 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 13° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 51 m of seating on the home side and 91 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
