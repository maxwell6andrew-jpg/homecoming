The game sets Portsmouth West High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 24 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 2 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 43° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 66 m of seating on the home side and 33 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
