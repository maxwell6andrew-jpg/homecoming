In the game, Bainbridge is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 19 buildings (mostly houses) and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 158° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 88 m of seating on the home side and 38 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
