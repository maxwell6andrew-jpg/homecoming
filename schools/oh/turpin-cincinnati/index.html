Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Turpin High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Cincinnati is set in hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 105 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 6 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 153° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 86 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
