Loreauville, Iberia Parish, Louisiana

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Loreauville High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Loreauville High School in bayou country, with tall dark trees by standing water; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 2 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 18 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 12° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
