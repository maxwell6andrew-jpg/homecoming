Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Catholic High School.

The game sets Catholic High School in a bayou town, with tall dark trees by standing water. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 16 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 50° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 56 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
