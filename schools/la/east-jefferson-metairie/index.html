The game sets East Jefferson High School in a coastal city with open water to the southwest, among palms; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 49 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 166° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 78 m for the home crowd and 78 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
