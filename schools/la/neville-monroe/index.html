Homecoming builds Monroe as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 9 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 12 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 143° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 56 m for the home crowd and 58 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
