Homecoming builds Mandeville as a beach suburb with open water to the southwest, among palms. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 11 buildings (mostly houses) and 29 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 23° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 44 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
