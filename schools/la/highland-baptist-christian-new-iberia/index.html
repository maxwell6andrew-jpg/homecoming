New Iberia, Iberia Parish, Louisiana

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Highland Baptist Christian School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds New Iberia as a bayou town, with tall dark trees by standing water. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 347 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 32° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 37 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
