In the game, Baton Rouge is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 12 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 36 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 52 m for the home crowd and 30 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
