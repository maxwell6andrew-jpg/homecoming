In the game, Thibodaux is a bayou suburb, with tall dark trees by standing water. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 6 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 10 streets and 31 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 13° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 62 m for the home crowd and 61 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
