Lafayette, Lafayette Parish, Louisiana

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lafayette High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Lafayette High School in a bayou city, with tall dark trees by standing water; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 21 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 18 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 120 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 141° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
