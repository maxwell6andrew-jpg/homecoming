Homecoming builds Haynesville as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 5 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 22 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 120 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 36° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 15 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
