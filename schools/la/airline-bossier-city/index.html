Homecoming builds Bossier City as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 133 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 9 streets. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 112 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
