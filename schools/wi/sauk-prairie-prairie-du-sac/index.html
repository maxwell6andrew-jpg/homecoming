Prairie du Sac, Sauk County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Sauk Prairie High.

Homecoming builds Prairie du Sac as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 229 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 15 streets. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 63 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
