Slinger, Washington County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Slinger High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Slinger as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 102 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 18° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 54 m of seating on the home side and 32 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
