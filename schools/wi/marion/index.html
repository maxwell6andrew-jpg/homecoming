Homecoming builds Marion as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap doesn't show a football field at Marion High yet, so the game places one at the school and builds the real neighbourhood around it: 16 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 20 streets within about 400 m.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
