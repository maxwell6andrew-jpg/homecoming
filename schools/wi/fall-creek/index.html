Fall Creek, Eau Claire County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Fall Creek High.

Homecoming builds Fall Creek as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 1 building within about 400 m of midfield and 12 streets. The game puts it around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 146° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
