Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Whitnall High.

Homecoming builds Greenfield as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 82 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 10 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 151° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 66 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
