Lodi, Columbia County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lodi High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Lodi is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 45 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 135° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
