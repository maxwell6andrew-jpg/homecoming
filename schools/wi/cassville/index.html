Cassville High football on your real field

Cassville, Grant County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Cassville High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Cassville High in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 88 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 14 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 70° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 71 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
