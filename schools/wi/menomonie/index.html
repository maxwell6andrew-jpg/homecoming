In the game, Menomonie is a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 149 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 44 streets and 158 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 110° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
