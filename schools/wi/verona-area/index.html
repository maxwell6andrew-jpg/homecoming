The game sets Verona Area High in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 39 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 26 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 16° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 66 m for the home crowd and 46 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
