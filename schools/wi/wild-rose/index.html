Wild Rose, Waushara County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Wild Rose High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Wild Rose is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 110 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 11 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 58° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 43 m for the home crowd and 28 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
