Elk Mound, Dunn County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Elk Mound High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Elk Mound is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 74 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 44° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 30 m for the home crowd and 20 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
