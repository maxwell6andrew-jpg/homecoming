Somerset, St. Croix County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Somerset High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Somerset High in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 31 buildings (mostly houses) and 20 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 133° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 41 m for the home crowd and 23 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
