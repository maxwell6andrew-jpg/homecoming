Spring Green, Sauk County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with River Valley High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Spring Green as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 143 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 7 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 135° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 38 m of seating on the home side and 33 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
