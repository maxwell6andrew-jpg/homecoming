Auburndale, Wood County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Auburndale High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Auburndale is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 68 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 6 streets and 335 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 21 m for the home crowd and 17 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
