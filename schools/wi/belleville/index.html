Belleville, Dane County, Wisconsin

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Belleville High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Belleville as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 89 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 8 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 125 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 42 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
