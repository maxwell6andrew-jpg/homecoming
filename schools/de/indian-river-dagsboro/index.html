Homecoming builds Dagsboro as a beach town with open water to the east, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 21 buildings (mostly houses) and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 14° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 42 m for the home crowd and 29 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
