The game sets Centennial High School in a suburb with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 310 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 47 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 154° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 108 m for the home crowd and 58 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
