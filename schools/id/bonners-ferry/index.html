Homecoming builds Bonners Ferry as a country town with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 122 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 14 streets and 43 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 40° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 49 m for the home crowd and 19 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
