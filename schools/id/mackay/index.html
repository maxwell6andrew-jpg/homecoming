The game sets Mackay Jr/Sr High School in a mountain town, with tall pines and rock; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 5 buildings (mostly houses) and 13 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 134° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 9 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
