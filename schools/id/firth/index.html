Homecoming builds Firth as a farm town with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 38 buildings (mostly houses) and 10 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 53° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 41 m of seating on the home side and 39 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
