Melba High School football on your real field

Melba, Canyon County, Idaho

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Melba High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Melba as a farm town with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 148 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 12 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 117° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 36 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
