In the game, Tucson is set in desert estates, with cactus, desert brush and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 74 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 4 streets and 34 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 89 m of seating on the home side and 60 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
