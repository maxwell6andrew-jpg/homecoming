Phoenix, Maricopa County, Arizona

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Scottsdale Christian Academy.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Scottsdale Christian Academy in a desert city with a mountain view, among palms and tall cactus; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 381 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 60 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 78 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
