Pima, Graham County, Arizona

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Pima High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Pima as desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 182 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 17 streets and 104 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 75 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
