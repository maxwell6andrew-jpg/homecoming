El Mirage, Maricopa County, Arizona

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Dysart High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds El Mirage as a desert suburb, with palms and tall cactus. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 178 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 21 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 55 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
