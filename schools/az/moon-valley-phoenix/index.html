Homecoming builds Phoenix as a desert suburb, with palms and tall cactus. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 317 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 32 streets. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 83 m of seating on the home side and 50 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
