The game sets San Manuel High School in desert ranch country, with cactus, desert brush and rock; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 77 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 9 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 157° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 63 m for the home crowd and 11 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
