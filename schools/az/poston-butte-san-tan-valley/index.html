San Tan Valley, Pinal County, Arizona

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Poston Butte High School.

Homecoming builds San Tan Valley as desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 12 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 7 streets and 30 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 97 m of seating on the home side and 28 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
