Morenci, Greenlee County, Arizona

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Morenci High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Morenci as a desert town with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 276 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 12 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 32 m for the home crowd and 34 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
