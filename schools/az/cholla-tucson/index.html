The game sets Cholla High School in a desert suburb with a mountain view, among palms and tall cactus. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 143 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 11 streets and 79 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 59 m for the home crowd and 46 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
