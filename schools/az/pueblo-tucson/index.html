Homecoming builds Tucson as a desert suburb with a mountain view, among palms and tall cactus. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 278 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 18 streets and 39 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 159° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 80 m for the home crowd and 108 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
