In the game, Yuma is a desert city, with palms and tall cactus. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 84 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 11 streets and 49 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 62 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
