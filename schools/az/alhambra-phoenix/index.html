The game sets Alhambra High School in a desert city, with palms and tall cactus; beyond the real streets it adds a big-city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 380 buildings (almost all of them houses), 17 streets and 92 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 81 m of seating on the home side and 55 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
