In the game, Phoenix is a desert suburb, with palms and tall cactus. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 312 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 17 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 4 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
