Homecoming builds Surprise as desert ranch country, with cactus, desert brush and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 160 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 31 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 145° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
