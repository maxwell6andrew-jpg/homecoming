In the game, Miami is set in desert ranch country with a mountain view, among cactus, desert brush and rock. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 15 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 3 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 74 m for the home crowd and 27 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
