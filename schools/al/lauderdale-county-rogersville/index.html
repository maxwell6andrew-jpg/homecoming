Rogersville, Lauderdale County, Alabama

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lauderdale County High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Rogersville as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 173 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 18 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 59 m for the home crowd and 52 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
