The game sets Central High School in a city, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 247 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 59 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 102 m of seating on the home side and 91 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
