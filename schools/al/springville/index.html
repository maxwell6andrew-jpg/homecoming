In the game, Springville is a country town. Around the stadium you get a country crossroads with a few farmsteads, barns, silos and crop fields, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap has the field but nothing mapped around it yet, so the game builds the neighbourhood from the town type.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 92 m for the home crowd and 52 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
