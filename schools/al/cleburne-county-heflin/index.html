Homecoming builds Heflin as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 21 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 8 streets and 43 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 119° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 92 m of seating on the home side and 92 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
