In the game, Douglas is a country town, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 13 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 73 m for the home crowd and 30 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
