Manchester, Carroll County, Maryland

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Manchester Valley High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Manchester is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 3 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 158° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 79 m of seating on the home side and 56 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
