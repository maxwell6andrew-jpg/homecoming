Baltimore, Baltimore city, Maryland

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with National Academy Foundation.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Baltimore is a coastal city with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (mostly houses), 76 streets and 93 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
