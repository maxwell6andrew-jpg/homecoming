The game sets Chesapeake High in a beach suburb with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 29 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 0 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 49 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
