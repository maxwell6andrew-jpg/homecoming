Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Richard Montgomery High.

Homecoming builds Rockville as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 231 buildings (mostly houses) and 59 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 135 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 29° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
