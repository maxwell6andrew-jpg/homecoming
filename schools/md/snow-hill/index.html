Snow Hill, Worcester County, Maryland

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Snow Hill High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Snow Hill High in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 19 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 14 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 148° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 56 m of seating on the home side and 26 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
