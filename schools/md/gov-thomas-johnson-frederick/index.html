Homecoming builds Frederick as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 224 buildings (mostly houses), 25 streets and 58 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 20° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 90 m of seating on the home side and 56 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
