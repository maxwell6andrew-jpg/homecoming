Glen Burnie, Anne Arundel County, Maryland

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with North County High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Glen Burnie is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 144 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 18° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
