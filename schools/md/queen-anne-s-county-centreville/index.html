Homecoming builds Centreville as a beach town with open water to the northwest, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 15 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 2 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 150° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 58 m of seating on the home side and 27 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
