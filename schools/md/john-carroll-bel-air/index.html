Bel Air, Harford County, Maryland

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with John Carroll School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Bel Air is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 199 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 20 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 161° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 68 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
