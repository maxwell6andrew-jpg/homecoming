Rockville, Montgomery County, Maryland

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Rockville High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Rockville as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 83 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 7 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 31° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 123 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
