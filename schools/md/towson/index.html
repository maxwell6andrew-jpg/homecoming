In the game, Towson is a city, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 419 buildings (mostly houses) and 18 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 18° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 65 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
