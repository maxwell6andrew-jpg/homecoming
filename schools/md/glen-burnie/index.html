The game sets Glen Burnie High in a beach suburb with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 49 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 64° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 57 m of seating on the home side and 41 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
