Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with North Point High School.

Homecoming builds Waldorf as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 40 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 7 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 156° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
