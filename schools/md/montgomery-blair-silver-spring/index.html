Silver Spring, Montgomery County, Maryland

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Montgomery Blair High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Silver Spring is a beach suburb with open water to the southeast, among pines and oaks. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 183 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 31 streets.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 71 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
