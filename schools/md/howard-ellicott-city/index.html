In the game, Ellicott City is a city, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 109 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 19 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 120° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 77 m for the home crowd and 29 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
