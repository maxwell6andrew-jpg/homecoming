The game sets Annapolis High in a beach suburb with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 87 buildings (mostly houses) and 5 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 122° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
