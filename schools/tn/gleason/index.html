Homecoming builds Gleason as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 41 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 19 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 40 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 151° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 18 m of seating on the home side and 16 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
