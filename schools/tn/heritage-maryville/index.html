Heritage High School football on your real field

Maryville, Blount County, Tennessee

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Heritage High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Heritage High School in the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 3 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 6 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 130° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 97 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
