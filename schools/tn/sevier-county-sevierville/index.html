Sevierville, Sevier County, Tennessee

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Sevier County High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Sevierville as the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 6 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 8 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 128° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 61 m for the home crowd and 35 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
