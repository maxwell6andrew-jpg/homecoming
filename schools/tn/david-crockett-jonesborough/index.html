The game sets David Crockett High School in the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 14 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 1 street. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 65° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 74 m of seating on the home side and 33 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
