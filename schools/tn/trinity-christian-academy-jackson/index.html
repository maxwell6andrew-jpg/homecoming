In the game, Jackson is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 8 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 0 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 168° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 52 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
