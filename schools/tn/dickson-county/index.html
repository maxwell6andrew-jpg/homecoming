In the game, Dickson is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 25 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 2 streets and 37 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 125 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 45° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
