Homecoming builds Nashville as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 176 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 12 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 165° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 95 m of seating on the home side and 90 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
