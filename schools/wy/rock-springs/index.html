Rock Springs, Sweetwater County, Wyoming

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Rock Springs High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Rock Springs as a desert town, with cactus, desert brush and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 319 buildings (mostly houses) and 17 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 69° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 78 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
