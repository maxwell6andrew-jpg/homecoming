Homecoming builds Wright as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 114 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 10 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 21° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 30 m for the home crowd and 21 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
