Homecoming builds White River Junction as a north woods town, with dense pine woods. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 161 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 23 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 25° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 29 m for the home crowd and 6 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
