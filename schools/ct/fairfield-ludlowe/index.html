Fairfield, Greater Bridgeport Region, Connecticut

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Fairfield Ludlowe High School.

Homecoming builds Fairfield as a beach suburb with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 91 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 14 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 15° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 71 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
