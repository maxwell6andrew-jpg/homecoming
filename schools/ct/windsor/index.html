Windsor, Capitol Region, Connecticut

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Windsor High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Windsor High School in a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 118 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 10 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 149° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 42 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
