The game sets Achievement First Bridgeport Academy in a coastal city with open water to the south, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 22 streets and 41 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 18 m for the home crowd and 18 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
