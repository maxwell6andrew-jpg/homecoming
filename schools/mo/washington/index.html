Washington, Franklin County, Missouri

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Washington High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Washington as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 100 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 39 streets and 88 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 68 m of seating on the home side and 23 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
