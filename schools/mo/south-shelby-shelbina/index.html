Shelbina, Shelby County, Missouri

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with South Shelby High.

Homecoming builds Shelbina as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 2 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 107° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
