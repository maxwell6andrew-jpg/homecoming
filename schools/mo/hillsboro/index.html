Hillsboro, Jefferson County, Missouri

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Hillsboro High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Hillsboro as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 8 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 3 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 110° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
