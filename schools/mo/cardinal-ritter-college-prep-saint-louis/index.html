The game sets Cardinal Ritter College Prep High School in a city, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 206 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 18 streets and 324 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 29° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 48 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
