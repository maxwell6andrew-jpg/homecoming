Mt Desert Island High School football on your real field

Bar Harbor, Hancock County, Maine

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Mt Desert Island High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Mt Desert Island High School in a beach town with open water to the east, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 11 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 1 street. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 69° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 64 m for the home crowd and 10 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
