Homecoming builds Portland as a coastal city with open water to the east, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap doesn't show a football field at Portland High School yet, so the game places one at the school and builds the real neighbourhood around it: 264 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 84 streets and 58 individually mapped trees within about 400 m.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
