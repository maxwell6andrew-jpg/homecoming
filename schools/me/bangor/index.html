The game sets Bangor High School in hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 107 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 13 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 62° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
