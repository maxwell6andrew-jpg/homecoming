Homecoming builds Portland as a beach suburb with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 25 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 22 streets and 29 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 127° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
