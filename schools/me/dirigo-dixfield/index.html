Dixfield, Oxford County, Maine

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Dirigo High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Dixfield as the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 77 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 17 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 40 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 27° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 21 m for the home crowd and 7 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
