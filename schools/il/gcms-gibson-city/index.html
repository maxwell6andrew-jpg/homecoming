Gibson City, Ford County, Illinois

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with GCMS High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Gibson City as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 12 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 12 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 42 m for the home crowd and 14 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
