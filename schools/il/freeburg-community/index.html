Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Freeburg Community High School.

Homecoming builds Freeburg as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 16 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 10 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 13° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
