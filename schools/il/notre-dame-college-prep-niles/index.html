The game sets Notre Dame College Prep in a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 39 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
