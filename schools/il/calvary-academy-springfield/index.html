Homecoming builds Springfield as a city, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 204 buildings (mostly houses), 22 streets and 26 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 81 m for the home crowd and 76 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
