Downs, McLean County, Illinois

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Tri-Valley High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Tri-Valley High School in a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 7 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 161° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 25 m of seating on the home side and 11 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
