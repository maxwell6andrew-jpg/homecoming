Mount Zion, Macon County, Illinois

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Mt Zion High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Mount Zion as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 3 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 3 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
