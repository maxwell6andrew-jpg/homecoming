The game sets Monticello High School in a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 16 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 18 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 83 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
