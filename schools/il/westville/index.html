Westville, Vermilion County, Illinois

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Westville High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Westville as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 12 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 10 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 58 m for the home crowd and 40 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
