Chester, Randolph County, Illinois

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Chester High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Chester as a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 20 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 15 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 135 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 154° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
