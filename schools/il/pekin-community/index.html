Pekin, Tazewell County, Illinois

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Pekin Community High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Pekin as hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 39 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 18 streets and 110 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 92 m for the home crowd and 91 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
