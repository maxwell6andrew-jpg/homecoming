Homecoming builds Oswego as hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 38 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 6 streets and 180 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 73 m of seating on the home side and 98 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
