Union City, Branch County, Michigan

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Union City High School.

Homecoming builds Union City as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 30 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 6 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 69° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 42 m of seating on the home side and 67 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
