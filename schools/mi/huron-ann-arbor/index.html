Homecoming builds Ann Arbor as hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 42 buildings (mostly houses) and 28 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 33° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 59 m for the home crowd and 38 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
