Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Muskegon Catholic Central.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Muskegon as a city, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 70 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 13 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 92 m of seating on the home side and 65 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
