Hastings, Barry County, Michigan

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Hastings High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Hastings is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 223 buildings (mostly houses) and 6 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 38 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
