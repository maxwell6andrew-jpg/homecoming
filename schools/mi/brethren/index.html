Brethren, Manistee County, Michigan

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Brethren High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Brethren is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 69 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 10 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 24 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
