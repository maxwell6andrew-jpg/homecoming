In the game, Detroit is a city, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 216 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 41 streets. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 153° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 44 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
