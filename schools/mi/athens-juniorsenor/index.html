Homecoming builds Athens as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 21 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 15 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 61° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 24 m of seating on the home side and 28 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
