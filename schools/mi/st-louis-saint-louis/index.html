In the game, Saint Louis is a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 38 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 17 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 75 m for the home crowd and 16 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
