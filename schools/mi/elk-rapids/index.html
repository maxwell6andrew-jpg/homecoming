Elk Rapids, Antrim County, Michigan

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Elk Rapids High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Elk Rapids as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 13 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 11 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 54 m of seating on the home side and 16 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
