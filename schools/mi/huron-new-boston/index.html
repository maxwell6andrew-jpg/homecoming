New Boston, Wayne County, Michigan

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Huron High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds New Boston as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 51 buildings (mostly houses) and 8 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 59 m of seating on the home side and 19 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
