The game sets King City High in a small farm town with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 396 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 26 streets and 308 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
