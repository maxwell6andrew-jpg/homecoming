Felton, Santa Cruz County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with San Lorenzo Valley High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Felton as a beach town with open water to the west, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 63 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 5 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 51 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
