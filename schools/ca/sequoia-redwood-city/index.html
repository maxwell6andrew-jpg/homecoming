Redwood City, San Mateo County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Sequoia High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Redwood City as a coastal city with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 313 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 96 streets and 93 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 45° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 99 m of seating on the home side and 34 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
