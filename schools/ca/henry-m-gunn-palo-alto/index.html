Homecoming builds Palo Alto as a suburb with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 204 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 13 streets and 80 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 154° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 71 m of seating on the home side and 72 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
