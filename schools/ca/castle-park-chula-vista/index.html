Homecoming builds Chula Vista as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 68 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 22 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 72° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
