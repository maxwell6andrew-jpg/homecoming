Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Valencia High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Placentia as a canyon suburb, with palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 416 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 41 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 14° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
