Homecoming builds National City as coastal hillside estates with open water to the southwest, among palms. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 9 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 47 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 72° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 33 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
