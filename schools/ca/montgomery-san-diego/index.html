In the game, San Diego is a beach suburb with a mountain view and open water to the northwest, among palms. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 42 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 23 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 92 m for the home crowd and 92 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
