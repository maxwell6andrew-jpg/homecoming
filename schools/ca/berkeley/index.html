Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Berkeley High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Berkeley is a coastal city with open water to the west, among pines and oaks. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (mostly houses), 93 streets and 110 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 73 m of seating on the home side and 78 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
