Homecoming builds San Francisco as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (almost all of them houses), 65 streets and 28 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 16° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 70 m for the home crowd and 41 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
