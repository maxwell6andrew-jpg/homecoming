Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Herbert Hoover High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Herbert Hoover High in a mountain city, with tall pines and rock; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 28 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 104° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
