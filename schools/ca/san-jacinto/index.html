Homecoming builds San Jacinto as a foothill suburb, with tall pines and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 2 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 28 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 135° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
