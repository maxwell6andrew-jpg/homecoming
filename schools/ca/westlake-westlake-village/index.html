Westlake Village, Ventura County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Westlake High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Westlake High in city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 14 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 8 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 109° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
