Redlands, San Bernardino County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Redlands Senior High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Redlands as a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 328 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 47 streets and 26 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 131° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 86 m for the home crowd and 56 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
