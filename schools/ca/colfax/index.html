Colfax High football on your real field

Colfax, Placer County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Colfax High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Colfax High in a farm town with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 23 buildings (mostly houses) and 8 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 44 m for the home crowd and 32 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
