In the game, El Cerrito is a beach suburb with open water to the southwest, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 40 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 69° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
