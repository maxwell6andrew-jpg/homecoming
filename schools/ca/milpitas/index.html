The game sets Milpitas High in a beach suburb with a mountain view and open water to the west, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 393 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 31 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
