Carmel, Monterey County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Carmel High.

In the game, Carmel is a beach suburb with a mountain view and open water to the west, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 234 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 24 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 103° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
