Winchester, Riverside County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Liberty High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Liberty High in a farm town with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 2 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 16 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
