In the game, San Diego is a country town, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 120 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 22 streets. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 62 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
