Simi Valley, Ventura County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Royal High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Royal High in a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 408 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 11 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 62 m for the home crowd and 78 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
