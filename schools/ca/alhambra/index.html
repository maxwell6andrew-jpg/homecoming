Alhambra, Los Angeles County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Alhambra High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Alhambra as a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 59 streets and 165 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 150° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
