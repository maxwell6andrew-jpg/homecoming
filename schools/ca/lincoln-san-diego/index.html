Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lincoln High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, San Diego is a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 8 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 48 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
