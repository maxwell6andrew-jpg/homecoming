Redding, Shasta County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Shasta High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Redding as a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 142 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 12 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 116° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 72 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
