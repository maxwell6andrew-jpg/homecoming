Corcoran, Kings County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Corcoran High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Corcoran High in a small farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 47 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 44 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
