Homecoming builds Woodland Hills as a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 411 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 31 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 117° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
