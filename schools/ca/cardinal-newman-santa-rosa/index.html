In the game, Santa Rosa is set in hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 87 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 13 streets and 175 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 163° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 53 m of seating on the home side and 39 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
