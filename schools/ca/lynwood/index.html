Lynwood High football on your real field

Lynwood, Los Angeles County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Lynwood High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Lynwood High in a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 33 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 19° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 112 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
