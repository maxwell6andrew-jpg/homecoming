Homecoming builds Morgan Hill as mountain estates, with tall pines and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 77 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 135° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 93 m for the home crowd and 75 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
