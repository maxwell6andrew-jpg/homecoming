The game sets Valley Center High in a country town with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 138 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 6 streets and 164 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 131° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
