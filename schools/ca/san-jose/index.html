San Jose, Santa Clara County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with San Jose High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets San Jose High in a city with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 24 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 149° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 56 m for the home crowd and 26 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
