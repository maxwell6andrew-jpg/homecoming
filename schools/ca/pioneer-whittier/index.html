Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Pioneer High.

Homecoming builds Whittier as a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 77 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 105° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 87 m for the home crowd and 68 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
