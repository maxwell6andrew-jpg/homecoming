Huntington Beach, Orange County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Marina High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Huntington Beach as a beach suburb with open water to the southwest, among palms. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 207 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 23 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
