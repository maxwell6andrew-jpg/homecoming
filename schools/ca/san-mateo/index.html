The game sets San Mateo High in a coastal city with open water to the north, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 406 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 28 streets and 78 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 49° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
