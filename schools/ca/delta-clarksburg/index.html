Clarksburg, Yolo County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Delta High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Delta High in a beach town with open water to the west, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 17 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 12 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 153° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
