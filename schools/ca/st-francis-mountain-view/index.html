Mountain View, Santa Clara County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with St. Francis High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets St. Francis High School in a suburb with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 215 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 25 streets and 100 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 96 m of seating on the home side and 85 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
