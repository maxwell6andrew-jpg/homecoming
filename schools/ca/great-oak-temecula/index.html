Temecula, Riverside County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Great Oak High.

Homecoming builds Temecula as a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 40 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 28 streets and 52 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 102° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 116 m of seating on the home side and 65 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
