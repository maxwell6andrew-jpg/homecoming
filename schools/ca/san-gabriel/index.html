San Gabriel, Los Angeles County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with San Gabriel High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds San Gabriel as a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 354 buildings (almost all of them houses), 13 streets and 350 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 120 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 159° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 68 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
