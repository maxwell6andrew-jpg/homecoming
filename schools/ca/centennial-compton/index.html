Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Centennial High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Centennial High in a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 32 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 110 m of seating on the home side and 80 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
