Sun Valley, Los Angeles County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with John H. Francis Polytechnic.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Sun Valley as a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 427 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 12 streets. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
