In the game, San Luis Obispo is set in city in the hills with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 413 buildings (mostly houses) and 33 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 140° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 51 m for the home crowd and 46 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
