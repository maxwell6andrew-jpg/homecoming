In the game, Palo Cedro is set in the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 31 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 13° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 73 m for the home crowd and 30 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
