Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Westmont High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Westmont High in city in the hills with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 24 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 74° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 50 m of seating on the home side and 50 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
