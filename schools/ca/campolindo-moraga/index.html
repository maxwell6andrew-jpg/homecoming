Homecoming builds Moraga as hillside estates, with dry brush, oaks and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 117 buildings (mostly houses), 10 streets and 110 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 124° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 100 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
