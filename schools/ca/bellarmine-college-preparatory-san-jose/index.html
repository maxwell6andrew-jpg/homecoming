Homecoming builds San Jose as a city with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 51 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 122° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 44 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
