Vista, San Diego County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Rancho Buena Vista High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Vista as a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 34 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
