Homecoming builds Napa as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 109 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 26 streets and 26 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 55° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 85 m of seating on the home side and 43 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
