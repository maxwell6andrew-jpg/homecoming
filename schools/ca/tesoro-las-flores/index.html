The game sets Tesoro High in ranch hills, with dry brush, oaks and rock; beyond the real streets it adds country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 12 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 0 streets and 45 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 102° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 65 m for the home crowd and 39 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
