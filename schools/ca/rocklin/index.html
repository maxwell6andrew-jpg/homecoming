Rocklin, Placer County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Rocklin High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Rocklin as a canyon suburb, with palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 238 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 15 streets and 288 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 73 m of seating on the home side and 56 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
