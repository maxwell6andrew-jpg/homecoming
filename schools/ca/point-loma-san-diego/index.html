The game sets Point Loma High in a coastal city with a mountain view and open water to the northwest, among palms; beyond the real streets it adds a big-city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 40 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 126° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 65 m of seating on the home side and 28 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
