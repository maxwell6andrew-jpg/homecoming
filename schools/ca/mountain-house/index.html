Mountain House, San Joaquin County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Mountain House High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Mountain House High in a beach suburb with a mountain view and open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 356 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 33 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 142° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
