Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with La Serna High.

In the game, Whittier is a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 137 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 16 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 115° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 93 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
