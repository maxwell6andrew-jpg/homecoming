Santa Ana, Orange County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Foothill High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Santa Ana as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 227 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 25 streets and 64 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 90 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 55 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
