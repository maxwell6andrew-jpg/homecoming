Newport Beach, Orange County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Corona del Mar High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Newport Beach is a beach suburb with a mountain view and open water to the south, among palms. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 34 streets and 140 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 73 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
