Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Woodbridge High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Woodbridge High in city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 208 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 71 streets and 26 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 39° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 28 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
