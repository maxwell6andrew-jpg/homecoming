Homecoming builds Thermal as a beach town with a mountain view and open water to the southeast, among palms; past the real streets you see country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 57 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 3 streets. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 59 m for the home crowd and 66 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
