Homecoming builds Saugus as a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 352 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 17 streets and 115 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 139° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 23 m of seating on the home side and 23 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
