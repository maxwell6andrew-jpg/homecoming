The game sets San Marino High in a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 448 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 23 streets and 54 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 77° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 75 m for the home crowd and 43 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
