The game sets Willow Glen High in a city with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 32 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 151° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 51 m for the home crowd and 26 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
