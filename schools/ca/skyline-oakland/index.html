Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Skyline High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Skyline High in hillside estates, with dry brush, oaks and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 140 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 15 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 140° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 79 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
