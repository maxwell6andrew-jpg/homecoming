Homecoming builds Boron as desert ranch country, with cactus, desert brush and rock; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 53 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 3 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 40 m of seating on the home side and 25 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
