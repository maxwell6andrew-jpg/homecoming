Lakeport, Lake County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Clear Lake High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Lakeport as a small town with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 108 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 11 streets and 33 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 52 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
