The game sets Thousand Oaks High in a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 294 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 18 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 120 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 75° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 77 m for the home crowd and 77 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
