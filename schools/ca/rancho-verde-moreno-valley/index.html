The game sets Rancho Verde High in a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 305 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 28 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 65 m of seating on the home side and 67 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
