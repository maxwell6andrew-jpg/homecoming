Fremont High football on your real field

Sunnyvale, Santa Clara County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Fremont High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Fremont High in a city with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 364 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 52 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 51 m for the home crowd and 39 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
