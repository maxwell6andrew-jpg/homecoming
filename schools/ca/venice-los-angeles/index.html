The game sets Venice Senior High in a coastal city with a mountain view and open water to the west, among palms; beyond the real streets it adds a big-city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 450 or more buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 40 streets. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 58° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 102 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
