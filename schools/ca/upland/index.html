Upland, San Bernardino County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Upland High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Upland as a suburb with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 273 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 24 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
