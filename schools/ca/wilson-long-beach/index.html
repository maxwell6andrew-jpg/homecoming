In the game, Long Beach is a coastal city with open water to the southwest, among palms. Past the real neighbourhood you see a big-city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 450 or more buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 51 streets. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 56 m of seating on the home side and 56 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
