Ferndale, Humboldt County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Ferndale High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Ferndale as a beach town with a mountain view and open water to the northwest, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 48 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
