Playa Del Rey, Los Angeles County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with St. Bernard High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Playa Del Rey is a coastal city with open water to the southwest, among palms. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 153 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 17 streets and 350 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 150° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 112 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
