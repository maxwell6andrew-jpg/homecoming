Paramount, Los Angeles County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Paramount High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Paramount High in a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 49 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 106 m of seating on the home side and 98 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
