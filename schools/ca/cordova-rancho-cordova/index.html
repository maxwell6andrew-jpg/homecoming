Homecoming builds Rancho Cordova as a suburb, with oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 41 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 15 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 136° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
