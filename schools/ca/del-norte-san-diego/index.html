Homecoming builds San Diego as a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 232 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 40 streets and 73 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 35° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 97 m for the home crowd and 59 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
