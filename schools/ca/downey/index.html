Downey, Los Angeles County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Downey High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Downey High in a city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 46 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 121° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 99 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
