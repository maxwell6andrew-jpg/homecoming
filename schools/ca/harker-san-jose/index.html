Homecoming builds San Jose as a city with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 240 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 68 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 117° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 44 m of seating on the home side and 18 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
