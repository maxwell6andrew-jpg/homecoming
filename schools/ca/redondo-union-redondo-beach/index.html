Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Redondo Union High.

In the game, Redondo Beach is a coastal city with a mountain view and open water to the west, among palms. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 39 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 67° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 54 m of seating on the home side and 29 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
