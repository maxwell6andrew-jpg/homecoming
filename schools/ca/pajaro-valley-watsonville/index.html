Homecoming builds Watsonville as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 17 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 6 streets and 72 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 61° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 80 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
