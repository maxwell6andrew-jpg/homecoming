Santa Rosa, Sonoma County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Montgomery High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Santa Rosa as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 379 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 28 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 72° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 64 m of seating on the home side and 64 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
