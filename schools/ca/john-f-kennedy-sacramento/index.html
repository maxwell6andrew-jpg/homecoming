Homecoming builds Sacramento as a beach suburb with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 75 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 31 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 160° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
