Homecoming builds Palo Alto as a beach suburb with a mountain view and open water to the east, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 448 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 44 streets and 43 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 48° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
