The game sets Beverly Hills High in a canyon suburb with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 441 buildings (mostly houses) and 39 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 149° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 98 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
