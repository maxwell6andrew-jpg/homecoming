Homecoming builds Ojai as a foothill suburb, with tall pines and rock. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 177 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses), 16 streets and 55 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 64° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 54 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
