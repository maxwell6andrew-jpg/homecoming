Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Tustin High.

The game sets Tustin High in a city, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 427 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 41 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs southeast to northwest, about 130° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 87 m of seating on the home side and 51 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
