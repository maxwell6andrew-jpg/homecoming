Homecoming builds Los Angeles as city in the hills with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush; past the real streets you see a big-city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 341 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 28 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 145° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
