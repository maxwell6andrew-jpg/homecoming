In the game, Mission Viejo is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 186 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 25 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 120 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 153° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 82 m of seating on the home side and 99 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
