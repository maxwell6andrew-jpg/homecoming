In the game, Salinas is a city with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (mostly houses) and 17 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 40 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 16° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 99 m for the home crowd and 79 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
