The game sets MetWest High School in a coastal city with open water to the southwest, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 298 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 62 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 138° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 176 m of seating on the home side and 91 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
