Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Christopher High.

Homecoming builds Gilroy as a suburb with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 132 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 16 streets and 25 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 110° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 82 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
