The game sets McClymonds High in a coastal city with open water to the west, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (mostly houses) and 47 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 16° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 71 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
