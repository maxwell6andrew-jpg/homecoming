Homecoming builds San Diego as city in the hills, with palms, oaks and brush; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 47 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 30 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 150° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 83 m for the home crowd and 21 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
