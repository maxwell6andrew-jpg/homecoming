The game sets Piedmont Hills High in a foothill suburb, with tall pines and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 348 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 32 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 62° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 49 m for the home crowd and 34 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
