Homecoming builds Novato as a beach suburb with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 320 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 16 streets and 52 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 52° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 72 m for the home crowd and 63 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
