PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Redwood High in hillside estates with a mountain view, among dry brush, oaks and rock. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 49 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 24 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 110 m of seating on the home side and 84 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
