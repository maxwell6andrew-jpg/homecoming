The game sets Ambassador High School in a city, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 418 buildings (mostly houses) and 49 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 104° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 81 m for the home crowd and 85 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
