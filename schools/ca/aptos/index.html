Aptos, Santa Cruz County, California

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Aptos High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Aptos High in a farm town with a mountain view, among oaks turning fall colors; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 15 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 2 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 143° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 61 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
