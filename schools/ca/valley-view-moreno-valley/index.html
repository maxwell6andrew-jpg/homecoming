Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Valley View High.

The game sets Valley View High in hillside estates with a mountain view, among dry brush, oaks and rock. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 56 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 26 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 70 m for the home crowd and 66 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
