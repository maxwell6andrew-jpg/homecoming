Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Hoover High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets Hoover High in city in the hills with a mountain view, among palms, oaks and brush; beyond the real streets it adds a big-city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 48 streets and 119 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 96 m for the home crowd and 82 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
