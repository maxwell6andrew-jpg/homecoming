In the game, Santa Monica is a coastal city with a mountain view and open water to the southwest, among palms. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 303 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 100 streets. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 136° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 80 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
