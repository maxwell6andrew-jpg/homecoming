Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Jordan High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Los Angeles as a big city with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a big-city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 320 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 38 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 75 m for the home crowd and 52 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
