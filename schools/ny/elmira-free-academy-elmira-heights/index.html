Homecoming builds Elmira Heights as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 103 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 9 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 105 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
