In the game, Staten Island is a beach suburb with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 386 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 26 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 40 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 80 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
