The game sets New Hyde Park Memorial High School in a city, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 25 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 165° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 69 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
