Hastings on Hudson, Westchester County, New York

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Hastings High School.

In the game, Hastings on Hudson is a beach suburb with open water to the north, among pines and oaks. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 410 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly houses) and 40 streets. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 48 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
