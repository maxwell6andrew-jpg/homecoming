In the game, Camden is a country town, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 7 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 2 streets and 27 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 167° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 46 m of seating on the home side and 25 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
