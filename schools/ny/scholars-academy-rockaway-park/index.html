Rockaway Park, Queens County, New York

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Scholars' Academy.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Rockaway Park as a beach suburb with open water to the east, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 161 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 39 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 69° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 49 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
