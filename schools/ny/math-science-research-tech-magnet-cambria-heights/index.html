Cambria Heights, Queens County, New York

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Math/Science Research/Tech Magnet.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Cambria Heights as a city, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 450 or more buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses), 41 streets and 46 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 71° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 50 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
