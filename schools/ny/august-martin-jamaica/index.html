The game sets August Martin High School in a coastal city with open water to the southwest, among pines and oaks; beyond the real streets it adds a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (almost all of them houses) and 33 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 150° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 110 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
