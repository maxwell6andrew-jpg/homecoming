In the game, Bemus Point is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 15 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 105° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 46 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
