Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with New Rochelle High School.

Homecoming builds New Rochelle as coastal hillside estates with open water to the south, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 8 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 25 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs southeast to northwest, about 143° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds huge stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
