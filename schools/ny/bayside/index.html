Bayside, Queens County, New York

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Bayside High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Bayside as a coastal city with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (almost all of them houses), 28 streets and 70 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 164° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 84 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
