The game sets Monroe-Woodbury High School in a suburb, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 4 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 29 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 13° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 54 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
