Homecoming builds Plainview as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 112 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 20 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium.

The artificial-turf field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 39 m of seating on the home side and 29 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
