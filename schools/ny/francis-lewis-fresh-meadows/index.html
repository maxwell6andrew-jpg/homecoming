Fresh Meadows, Queens County, New York

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Francis Lewis High School.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Fresh Meadows as a coastal city with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see a city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 417 buildings (almost all of them houses) and 79 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 103° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 90 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
