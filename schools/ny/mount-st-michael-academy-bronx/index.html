The game sets Mount St. Michael Academy in a big city, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds a big-city skyline. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 450 or more buildings (almost all of them houses) and 18 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 73° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 42 m of seating on the home side and 30 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
