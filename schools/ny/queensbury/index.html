The game sets Queensbury Senior High School in a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 31 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 3 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 38 m for the home crowd and 30 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
