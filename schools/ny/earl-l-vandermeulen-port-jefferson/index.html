Homecoming builds Port Jefferson as a beach suburb with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 223 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 21 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 65° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 48 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
