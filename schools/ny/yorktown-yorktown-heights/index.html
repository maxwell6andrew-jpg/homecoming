Homecoming builds Yorktown Heights as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 8 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 0 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 39 m for the home crowd and 29 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball.
