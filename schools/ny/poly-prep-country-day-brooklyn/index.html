Homecoming builds Brooklyn as a beach suburb with open water to the north, among pines and oaks. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 169 buildings (mostly houses), 43 streets and 345 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 107° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 36 m of seating on the home side and 17 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
