Homecoming builds Batavia as a small town, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 279 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 14 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 120° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds small stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
