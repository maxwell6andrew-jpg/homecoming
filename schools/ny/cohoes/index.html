In the game, Cohoes is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 1 building within about 400 m of midfield and 14 streets. The game puts it around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 65° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 66 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
