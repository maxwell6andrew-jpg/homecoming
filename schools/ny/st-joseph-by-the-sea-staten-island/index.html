Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with St. Joseph by the Sea High School.

Homecoming builds Staten Island as coastal hillside estates with open water to the southeast, among pines and oaks. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 185 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (almost all of them houses) and 31 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 149° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 87 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds huge visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
