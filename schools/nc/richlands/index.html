In the game, Richlands is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 20 buildings inside a 400 m radius (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 9 streets. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
