In the game, Charlotte is a city, with leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see a city skyline. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 243 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 21 streets and 184 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 60 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 23° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 55 m for the home crowd and 79 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
