Homecoming builds Chapel Hill as hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 102 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 17 streets and 212 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 45 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 127° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds big stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
