Black Mountain, Buncombe County, North Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Owen High.

In the game, Black Mountain is a mountain town, with tall pines and rock. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 43 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 2 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 59 m for the home crowd and 43 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
