Homecoming builds Shelby as a north woods town, with dense pine woods. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 84 buildings (mostly houses), 11 streets and 27 individually mapped trees, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 15° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
