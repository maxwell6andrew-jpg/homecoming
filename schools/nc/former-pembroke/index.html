Homecoming builds Pembroke as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 55 buildings (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 16 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 63 m for the home crowd and 45 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
