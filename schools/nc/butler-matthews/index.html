Homecoming builds Matthews as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 114 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses), 4 streets and 85 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 75 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 35° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 41 m of seating on the home side and 52 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
