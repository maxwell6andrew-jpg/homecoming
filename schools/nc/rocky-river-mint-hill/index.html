Mint Hill, Mecklenburg County, North Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Rocky River High School.

Homecoming builds Mint Hill as the north woods, with dense pine woods; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 13 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 1 street. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 96 m for the home crowd and 50 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
