Homecoming builds Yanceyville as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 1 building and 2 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-northeast to west-southwest, about 57° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 33 m of seating on the home side and 25 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
