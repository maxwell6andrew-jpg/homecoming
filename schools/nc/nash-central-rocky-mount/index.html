Rocky Mount, Nash County, North Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Nash Central High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Rocky Mount as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 33 buildings (mostly houses) and 1 street, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 27° on the compass. No grandstands are mapped, so the game builds mid-size stands to match the school's enrollment.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
