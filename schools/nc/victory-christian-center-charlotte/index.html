Homecoming builds Charlotte as a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 89 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 31 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 130 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 59 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds small visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
