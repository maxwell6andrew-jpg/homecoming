Homecoming builds Holly Ridge as a beach town with open water to the east, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see country roads. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: only 2 buildings inside a 400 m radius (almost all of them houses) and 18 streets. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 30° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 48 m of seating on the home side and 42 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
