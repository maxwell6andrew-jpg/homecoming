The game sets Western Alamance High in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap shows 1 street around the field but no buildings yet, so in the game that street runs past empty lots.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 35 m of seating on the home side and 64 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
