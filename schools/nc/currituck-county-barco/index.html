Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Currituck County High.

Homecoming builds Barco as a beach town with open water to the northeast, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 7 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments) and 1 street. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs northeast to southwest, about 50° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 80 m for the home crowd and 13 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
