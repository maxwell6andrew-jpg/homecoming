Andrews High football on your real field

Andrews, Cherokee County, North Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Andrews High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

In the game, Andrews is set in the north woods with a mountain view, among dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 190 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings), 12 streets and 197 individually mapped trees. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 80 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 48 m of seating on the home side and 40 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
