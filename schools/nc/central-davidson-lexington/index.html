The game sets Central Davidson High in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 10 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 2 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 105° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 75 m for the home crowd and 57 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
