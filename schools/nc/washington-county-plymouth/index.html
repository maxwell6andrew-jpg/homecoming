Plymouth, Washington County, North Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Washington County High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Plymouth as a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 137 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 12 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 50 m from midfield.

The field runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 159° on the compass. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 64 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds mid-size visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
