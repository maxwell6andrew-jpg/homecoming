Homecoming builds Erwin as a small town, with leafy shade trees. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 16 buildings (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 3 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 95 m from midfield.

The field runs northeast to southwest, about 50° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 71 m of seating on the home side and 43 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
