Lenoir, Caldwell County, North Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with West Caldwell High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

The game sets West Caldwell High in a country town with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 1 building within about 400 m of midfield and 3 streets. The game puts it around the stadium.

The field sits inside a running track and runs south-southeast to north-northwest, about 164° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 70 m of seating on the home side and 45 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
