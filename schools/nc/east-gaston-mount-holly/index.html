Homecoming builds Mount Holly as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap shows 2 streets around the field but no buildings yet, so in the game those streets run past empty lots.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 14° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 54 m of seating on the home side and 37 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
