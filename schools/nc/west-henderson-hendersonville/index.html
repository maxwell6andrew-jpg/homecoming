In the game, Hendersonville is a country town with a mountain view, among leafy shade trees. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows only 1 building and 3 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 100 m from midfield.

The artificial-turf field runs northeast to southwest, about 47° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 84 m for the home crowd and 57 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
