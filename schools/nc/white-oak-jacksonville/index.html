In the game, Jacksonville is a farm town, with oaks turning fall colors. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads and crop fields. Within about 400 m of the 50-yard line, OpenStreetMap shows 28 buildings (mostly houses) and 4 streets, and that is the neighbourhood you see from the field. The closest building is about 110 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 144° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 62 m for the home crowd and 57 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
