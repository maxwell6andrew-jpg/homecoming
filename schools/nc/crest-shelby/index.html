The game sets Crest High in a country town, with leafy shade trees; beyond the real streets it adds country roads and crop fields. OpenStreetMap shows 3 streets around the field but no buildings yet, so in the game those streets run past empty lots.

The field sits inside a running track and runs east-southeast to west-northwest, about 122° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 53 m for the home crowd and 39 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
