Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Apex Friendship High.

In the game, Apex is set in the north woods, with dense pine woods. Past the real neighbourhood you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 33 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 83 m for the home crowd and 33 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
