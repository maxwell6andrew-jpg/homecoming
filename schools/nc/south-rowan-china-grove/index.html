Homecoming builds China Grove as a country town, with leafy shade trees; past the real streets you see country roads and crop fields. The real neighbourhood comes from OpenStreetMap: 34 buildings inside a 400 m radius (mostly bigger buildings like classrooms, shops, offices or apartments), 2 streets and 98 individually mapped trees. The closest building is about 55 m from midfield.

The field runs east-west. That is unusual: most fields point north-south to keep a setting sun out of the players' eyes. OpenStreetMap has one grandstand, about 89 m long. The game makes it the home side and adds big visitor bleachers across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
