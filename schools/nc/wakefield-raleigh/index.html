The game sets Wakefield High in hillside estates, with oaks turning fall colors. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 119 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (mostly houses) and 8 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 85 m from midfield.

The field runs north-northeast to south-southwest, about 16° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 74 m for the home crowd and 27 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
