Hertford, Perquimans County, North Carolina

Homecoming is an independent football video game played on this field. Not affiliated with Perquimans County High.

PLAY NOWfree, in your browser

Homecoming builds Hertford as a beach town with open water to the east, among pines and oaks; past the real streets you see country roads. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced only 6 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 5 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 70 m from midfield.

The field runs north-south, the usual layout, which keeps a low sun out of the players' eyes. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 48 m of seating on the home side and 14 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
