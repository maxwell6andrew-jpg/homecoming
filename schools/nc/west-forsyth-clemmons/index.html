In the game, Clemmons is a suburb, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 93 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 13 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 65 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 132° on the compass. Grandstands are mapped on both sides: about 75 m of seating on the home side and 62 m across the field.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
