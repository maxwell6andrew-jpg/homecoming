In the game, Winston-Salem is set in hillside estates, with leafy shade trees. OpenStreetMap volunteers have traced 20 buildings within about 400 m of midfield (a mix of houses and bigger buildings) and 12 streets. The game puts them all around the stadium. The closest building is about 115 m from midfield.

The field sits inside a running track and runs southeast to northwest, about 144° on the compass. Both sidelines have mapped stands, roughly 35 m for the home crowd and 29 m for visitors.

How it plays

You play offense: pick the play, take the snap, make the throw or find the hole. You don't play defense. It's simulated, on purpose: when the other team has the ball, you make one call from the sideline and your defense plays the series. That keeps every snap you control about moving the ball. Free in your browser on phone, desktop or controller, with no download.
